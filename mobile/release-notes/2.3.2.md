Oruinwan 2.3.2 makes encrypted messaging work for everyone, every time, with a fresh light look. It installs as a quick update.

- Messages: new members can message anyone, and anyone can message them, straight away. No more "conversation key missing", and no need to sign out and back in with your password
- Google sign-in: no password prompt to unlock your messages. Older accounts switch to automatic unlock the next time they sign in
- Channels and DMs repair themselves: if your key for a conversation is missing or out of date, the app gets a fresh one in the background
- Threads: @name, @here and @channel work in thread replies and notify people
- A new light look by default, with the logo on a clear background. Dark mode is still in Settings → Appearance
