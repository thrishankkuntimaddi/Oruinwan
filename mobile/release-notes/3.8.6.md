Oruinwan 3.8.6 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Clearer wording about encryption:** messages are encrypted on your device, and the apps now say exactly that everywhere. Oruinwan's server keeps a protected copy of each conversation key (so AI agents can answer and you can recover your account), so we no longer call it "end-to-end".
- **Contact addresses that work:** the Contact, Security, Terms and other pages now point to admin@poelys.com, so your message reaches us.
- **Clearer integration errors:** Jira and Confluence errors no longer show technical exception names.
