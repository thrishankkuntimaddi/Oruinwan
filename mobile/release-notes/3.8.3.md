Oruinwan 3.8.3 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Oruinwan AI in every header:** the AI button sits next to search on every screen (chats, threads, tasks, notes, calendar, knowledge, files and more) and travels between them as you move around, a different way each time. Prefer the floating button? Settings → Oruinwan AI → Floating AI button.
- **One button for emoji, GIFs and stickers:** tabs inside, like your phone's keyboard, with your recent emoji first.
- **Replies to photos show the photo:** a small preview instead of a file name, on the phone and the web.
- **Stickers in a tap:** cut-outs find the person or thing on their own, and you can press and hold any photo in a chat → Make a sticker.
- **Files:** a file shared in several chats appears once, photos show their pictures, and you can switch between a list and a grid.
- **Tablets:** every place is in the bottom bar (no more drawer), the list column is roomier, and Home's summary cards fit.
- **Web:** the workspace picture sits properly on its cover in workspace settings.
