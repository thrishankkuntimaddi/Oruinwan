Oruinwan 3.3.1 fixes mentions and unread counts, and makes starting a conversation work like Slack. It's a quick update on phones and the desktop app; no reinstall.

- **Mentions with full names:** picking "Pakanati Shiva Kumar Goud" mentions the whole name, not just the first word. The person is notified, offers to add them to a group or channel work, and older messages show the full name too.
- **New message with a To: bar (web):** add one person and your chat with them opens right below; add more and the group with exactly those people opens. If there's no such group yet, it says so and starts it with your first message.
- **Unread counts stay cleared:** a chat you've read no longer shows its old count when you come back, on the web and the phone.
- **iPhone notifications:** one notification per chat; a new message replaces the earlier one instead of stacking.
