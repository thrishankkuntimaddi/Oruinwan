Oruinwan 3.10.2 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Huddles and calls:** the call screen is solid again. The page behind it no longer shows through between the video tiles, and the people and meeting panels are frosted glass.
- **More drawer:** the places above the tab bar are laid out five across, two rows, like the dock.
- **Conversations on the web:** the date between days is a floating glass label, with no line running through it.
- **Huddles on the web:** the side panel switches Live now, Upcoming and Past, and the page shows the list, so nothing appears twice.
- **Tablets held upright** use the phone layout (one screen at a time, the phone's tab bar and drawer), which is easier to use one-handed. Landscape keeps the list and open item side by side.
