Oruinwan 2.0.1 makes meetings and navigation consistent everywhere.

- Every meeting has the same tabs before, during and after the call: Overview, Agenda, Notes, Transcript, Summary & actions, Whiteboard and Brainstorm
- Quick huddles from a chat get the same meeting page and recap as scheduled meetings
- Notes, action items and the summary are kept in one place, so what you write during a call is on the meeting page afterwards
- Huddles shows Live now, Upcoming and Past; when a call ends you get "Meeting ended · View recap"
- Roadmap is now part of Notes, and Design reviews is in Tasks → Planning
- Tasks keep the view you picked (list, board, sprint, timeline, workload); Trash and Time report on phones and tablets
- The whiteboard and brainstorm notes use the Oruinwan colours in light and dark
- Install on a Mac: open the DMG, drag Oruinwan to Applications, then the first time go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway
