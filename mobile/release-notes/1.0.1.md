Reliability and security fixes.

- In-app updates now work on Android 9 and 10 (and early Android 13 builds)
- A brief server outage no longer signs you out
- Signing out while the app refreshes your session now fully ends it
- Live updates reconnect on their own after your session renews or the app returns to the foreground
- Oruinwan AI renews an expired session instead of showing an error
- New encryption keys are no longer readable by the server without your password
- "Clear this device" also removes cached encryption keys
- Phones with an outdated Android System WebView now see how to update it instead of a blank screen
