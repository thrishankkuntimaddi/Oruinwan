Oruinwan 2.3.3 fixes app updates on Android and brings the phone up to par with the desktop. Android phones on 2.3.1 or 2.3.2 install this one as a full download, once; quick updates work from then on.

- Android updates: quick updates no longer fail with "That update isn't ready". If a quick update ever can't be applied, the app now offers the full download instead
- Chat on the phone: a ⋮ menu with everything the desktop has. DMs: call, search, reminders, pinned, saved, profile, mute, block, clear and delete. Channels: huddle, poll, search, threads, reminders, pinned, saved, info, members and mute
- Message box on the phone: bold, italic, strikethrough, code, links, lists and quotes, plus an emoji picker
- Message info: see who read your message and when, on the phone and the web. Read receipts are now recorded reliably
- Oruinwan AI: @mention people in AI chats. The AI can message them for you (it shows exactly what it sent) and search your recent conversation with them
- Files: lists every file shared with you in the workspace, from channels, DMs and uploads, with filters, search, preview and "Go to message"
- Knowledge: easier to find (back in the side bar), clear errors instead of an empty page, and a welcome page to write, import or organise
- Workspace settings for owners and admins: default channels, plan & usage, AI, integrations, transfer ownership, archive and restore. Members can invite people only when an admin turns that on
