The first Oruinwan app for Android.

- **Home** with your unread messages, mentions and tasks due today
- **Chats**: channels and direct messages, threads, reactions
- **Oruinwan AI** in the centre of the tab bar
- **Tasks** as a list or board, and **Notes** with a rich editor
- Light, dark and system themes
- In-app updates: new versions install over this one and keep your data
- Explore the built-in demo workspace without an account

Push notifications and file uploads arrive in a later update.
