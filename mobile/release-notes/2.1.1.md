Oruinwan 2.1.1 finishes the move to the Oruinwan name. It installs as a quick update, no reinstall needed.

- The last places that still said Chttrix now say Oruinwan
- Your saved settings, notes groups, AI chat history and encryption keys move to the new names automatically the first time the app opens
- Developers: webhooks are signed with X-Oruinwan-Signature (X-Oruinwan-Event, X-Oruinwan-Delivery) and the SDK sends X-Oruinwan-SDK. The old ChttrixSDK name has been removed
- Links point to the renamed GitHub repository, github.com/thrishankkuntimaddi/Oruinwan
