Oruinwan 3.8.2 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Put the AI button where you like:** drag it to any corner of the screen (top or bottom, left or right). It snaps into place, is remembered, and a chat's AI button moves to the same side.
- **The AI button stays on screen:** its flight between screens no longer swings past the edge.
