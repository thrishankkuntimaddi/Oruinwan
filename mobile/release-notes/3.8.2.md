Oruinwan 3.8.2 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Choose where the AI button sits (optional):** switch on Settings → Oruinwan AI → **Move the AI button**, then drag it to any corner of the screen. It snaps into place, is remembered, and a chat's AI button moves to the same side. Off (the default), it stays bottom right.
- **The AI button stays on screen:** its flight between screens no longer swings past the edge.
