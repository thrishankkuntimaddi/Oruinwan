Oruinwan is free and open source. 2.1 brings the free plan, easier sign-in and one-click updates.

- Free plan: up to 4 devices, create 2 + join 2 workspaces, 10 members and 10 channels per workspace, huddles up to 10 people and 30 minutes, 300 active tasks, 50 AI requests a day. See Settings → Plan & usage. Pro and Enterprise are coming soon
- Sign in with a QR code: scan your computer's code with your phone, or sign in a new phone by scanning the code on your computer
- On a 5th device you choose which device to sign out, or sign out everywhere and continue
- Two-step sign-in can be approved from a device you're already signed in on, or with an email code
- Messages unlock automatically after signing in with Google
- Encrypted Google Drive backup in a hidden app folder, with restore and account switching (Settings → Backup)
- Updates: Settings → About & updates checks for new versions; the desktop and Android apps update in one click and restart
- New pricing page and landing page
- Install on a Mac: open the DMG, drag Oruinwan to Applications, then the first time go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway
