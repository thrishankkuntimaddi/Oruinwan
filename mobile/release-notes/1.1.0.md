Oruinwan now runs on your computer and in the browser, not just your phone.

- New apps for Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux
- A laptop layout for wide screens: a sidebar instead of the tab bar, with your list and the open conversation, task or note side by side
- The open item is highlighted in its list, and sheets open as centred dialogs
- With a keyboard: Enter sends (Shift+Enter for a new line), Ctrl+K or ⌘K opens search, Escape closes and goes back
- With a mouse: right-click a message, or hover it, for its actions
- Phones keep the layout they have today
