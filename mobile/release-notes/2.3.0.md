Oruinwan 2.3 is a stability release: calls that ring, apps that don't crash, and a calmer layout. It's a full app update for the desktop and Android apps.

- Calls ring: DM and group calls ring every device with Accept and Decline, also when the Android app is closed (lock-screen call notification). The caller sees "Declined" or "No answer", missed calls show in notifications, and answering on one device stops the ringing on the others
- Channel huddles show a "Join" banner with a short chime instead of ringing everyone
- A 1:1 call ends for both people when one hangs up, like a phone call
- Android: fixed the app closing after allowing the microphone; permissions are asked when needed with Open settings if they were turned off; Settings → Notifications → Calls shows what's allowed. If the app ever closes unexpectedly, the next launch offers to copy the details for support
- Desktop app: microphone and camera work in calls on macOS, "Open meeting page" stays in the app, and an incoming call brings the window forward with Accept and Decline
- Group DMs: start a conversation with several people, rename it, add or remove people, and call the whole group
- A crash in one part of the app no longer breaks the whole app, and a busy server no longer signs you out
- Profile pictures no longer show as broken images
- Phone: compact Home with small counters, better spacing for recent notes, Settings search, Help center, Contact support and What's new
- Oruinwan AI keeps your chat history across your devices, with rename and delete
- Meeting tabs scroll instead of overlapping; Activity moved out of the desktop sidebar (it's in notifications), and stays on the phone's Home
- Limits: screen sharing from an Android phone isn't available yet (you can watch others' screens)
