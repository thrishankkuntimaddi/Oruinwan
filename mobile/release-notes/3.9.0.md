Oruinwan 3.9.0 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Private chats (where your server turns them on):** a one-to-one chat that only your and the other person's devices can read, not Oruinwan's servers and not workspace admins. Compare the safety number with the other person to be sure. Private chats have no AI or search, and messages stay on the devices they were sent to.
- **A more focused app:** sprints, milestones, the roadmap and design reviews are switched off on oruinwan.poelys.com for now, so Tasks and Notes stay simple. Nothing is deleted; they can come back.
- **Task moves match on every device:** on the phone you can now move a task from Backlog or To do straight to Done or Blocked, like on the web, and the phone's board has a Backlog column.
- **Mentions with full names:** in thread replies and slash commands on the web, "@Ana Silva" now notifies Ana, not just "@Ana".
- **Integrations:** Bitbucket repository lists work again (Bitbucket retired an old address), the Google Meet connect check works with the calendar.events permission, the Slack setup lists every permission it needs, and Notion search shows pages only.
