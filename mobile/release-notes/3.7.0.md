Oruinwan 3.7.0 adds paying for Pro online and fixes meetings on the phone. It's a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Pay for Pro online:** workspace owners can upgrade on *Plans and pricing* by card, UPI or net banking (Razorpay). It's billed per member, everyone in the workspace gets Pro, and you can stop renewing at any time.
- **A real whiteboard:** the board is white with dark ink. A new full-screen button turns it sideways to use the whole phone, and drawing downwards no longer closes the meeting or shrinks the call.
- **Brainstorm you can arrange:** drag notes with your finger to group them, or tap **Tidy up**. Everyone sees them move.
- **Meeting notes fill the screen** instead of a small box.
- **Find your recording:** a recorded meeting shows its recording on Overview, with **Open file**.
- **Joining calls on weak connections:** the app waits a few seconds for the connection to come back instead of failing at once.
- **Very old app versions** (before 3.2.0) are now asked to update before they connect.
