Oruinwan 3.8.0 brings stickers like you know them, and fixes for presence and photos. On Android it's a full download this time (new WhatsApp support); on computers it's a quick update.

- **Cut-out stickers:** choose **Cut out** and tap the person or thing to keep; the background goes. Put it on nothing, a colour or a picture, with a white outline and a caption. It runs on your phone (one-time download of the cut-out tool, about 45 MB).
- **Add to WhatsApp:** use a sticker pack in WhatsApp, or share any sticker to Instagram, Facebook and other apps.
- **Packs that work:** create a new pack while saving, move stickers between packs, rename or delete packs, import pictures, .zip or .wastickers packs, and find what you used in **Recent**. Press and hold a sticker to copy, share or save it.
- **Photos and videos:** swipe sideways with your finger, swipe down to close, tap to hide the bars, double tap to zoom.
- **Who's online:** people now show as offline or away when they leave, instead of staying green.
- **AI button:** it flies to the top of a chat when you open it, and back when you close it.
