Oruinwan 3.6.0 introduces plans: Free, Pro and Enterprise. It's a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Free stays free:** every feature, with the same limits as before.
- **Pro, $5 per member a month** (billed yearly, $6 monthly): owner + 100 members, unlimited channels and notes, calls of 25 people for up to 5 hours, 10 signed-in devices and more AI. Everyone in a Pro workspace gets Pro limits there. Choose **Upgrade to Pro** in Settings → Plan & usage.
- **Enterprise:** we set Oruinwan up for your company, on your servers or ours, with single sign-on, your domain and onboarding. Choose **Talk to us**.
- **Clearer limits:** when you reach a limit, Oruinwan names your plan and what you can do next.
- **AI with your own key** stays unlimited on every plan.
