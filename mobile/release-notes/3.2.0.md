Oruinwan 3.2.0 makes channels and DMs work like a team chat should, puts the phone app on the website, and fixes calls. Phones and the desktop take a full update once (new app links); after that, quick updates as usual.

- **Calls:** adding people to a call no longer drops it. People you add are rung, join, and everyone stays connected.
- **Channels:** new members read the whole history, threads included. @mention anyone in the workspace; if they aren't in the channel, you're offered **Add to channel**.
- **DMs and groups:** mention someone else in a 1:1 DM to start a group with the three of you. Tick several people in New message to open (or start) their group. Group names stay short: "Ana, Ben and 3 others".
- **Read receipts** on the web: one tick when sent, two when read, live.
- **Send later** updates live and says why a message couldn't be sent.
- **Oruinwan AI:** the AI button opens a quick window right where you are; its face follows your touch. Ask AI about a chat on the web too. Agents answer in threads.
- **Tasks:** open full width on the web, link tickets (relates to, blocks, duplicates), and a message that became a task shows its task.
- **Workspaces:** a profile with logo, cover, tagline, website and location. New members land in the default channels however they're added; admins invite from the phone.
- **People who left** keep their name, tagged Former member or Deactivated.
- **Email buttons** open the Oruinwan app when it's installed, otherwise the browser.
- **The website on a phone** now shows the phone app, with notifications and calls.
- **Fixes:** reacting no longer opens the thread on the phone; DMs you never wrote in stay out of the list; the Ask AI pill gets out of the way.
