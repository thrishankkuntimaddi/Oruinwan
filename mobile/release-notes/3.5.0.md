Oruinwan 3.5.0 brings stickers and GIFs to every app. It's a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Stickers and GIFs:** a new sticker button next to emoji in the message box. Send your own stickers, or search GIFs and animated stickers from GIPHY.
- **Make your own:** turn any photo into a sticker: crop it, pick a shape (rounded, circle or no frame), add a caption. GIFs and see-through pictures can be added as they are, so animated stickers stay animated.
- **Save what others send:** press and hold (phone) or open the message menu (web and desktop) on a sticker, GIF or photo → Save as sticker.
- **Your packs, everywhere:** group stickers into packs. Your stickers follow you across the web, desktop and phone apps.
