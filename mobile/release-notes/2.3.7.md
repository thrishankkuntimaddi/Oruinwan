Oruinwan 2.3.7 brings a new phone layout, working calls for everyone and an assistant that does the work. On the phone this is a full download (once), so invitation and message links open in the app; on the desktop it is a quick update.

- New phone tabs: Home, Chats, Tasks, Notes and Activity, with AI, Search and your profile at the top of every tab
- Home shows Unread, your tasks, huddles and saved items, plus a quick "Jump to" for recent chats
- Chats keep every conversation in its own section, unread ones highlighted; new message is at the bottom right
- Calls ring first and start when the other person answers; cameras and shared screens reach everyone; switching cameras on the phone works
- See who reacted to a message; poll results update live for everyone
- @ai can start threads, reply in them, create tasks and notes, and set reminders
- Share or copy a message or a whole thread, with a link
- Threads: follow, add people, and admins can lock a thread
- Every task has a discussion that records status, assignee and due-date changes
- Scheduling warns when someone is already busy, suggests free times and shows each meeting's priority
- The meeting agenda saves as you type and appears for everyone; the whiteboard draws right away
- One account per person, whether you sign in with Google or a password
