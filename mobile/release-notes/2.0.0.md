Oruinwan 2.0 brings the full workspace to every device: web, desktop, phones and tablets.

- Tablets and iPads now use the Oruinwan app layout, stretched into two or three columns, with push notifications and keyboard shortcuts
- Chats: polls, checklists, remind me later, saved items, edit history, voice messages, smart replies, translation, @mentions, and AI summaries of channels and threads
- Threads: follow, mark resolved, participants and summaries
- Notes and knowledge pages are edited live together, with everyone's cursor shown; the meeting whiteboard is live too
- Roadmap: plan by quarter with milestones and progress
- Design reviews: pin comments on images, videos and PDFs, then approve or request changes
- Meetings: recording, live captions, an AI assistant during the call, and summaries with action items; "Find a time" checks everyone's calendar
- Tasks: subtasks, dependencies, repeating tasks, templates, sprints, milestones, timeline, workload and time tracking
- Automations: multi-step workflows, schedules, run history (for example: pull request merged → post in #engineering → create a release task)
- Integrations with a personal token: GitLab, Bitbucket, Linear, CI/CD, Slack and Teams bridges, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Notion, Confluence, Google Meet, Zapier, Make, n8n and incoming webhooks
- Search shortcuts: from:@name, in:#channel, before:, after:, has:file, is:pinned, type:task
- Messages you send while offline are sent automatically when you're back online
- Reply to messages straight from notifications (desktop and iPhone)
- Desktop: tray icon, keeps running in the background, launch at login
- Security: company sign-in (SAML and OIDC), device management, workspace retention policies, encrypted backups
- Developers: versioned public API (/api/public/v1) with a full reference, app submissions and a community template gallery
- Install on a Mac: open the DMG, drag Oruinwan to Applications, then the first time go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway
