Oruinwan 3.8.1 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **The AI button in a chat has its face:** eyes that follow your touch, a smile when you tap it, a curious look while it thinks, and a happy (or sad) face when it answers.
- **A different flight every time:** the AI button flies to a chat by a new path each time (an arc, a loop, hops, a zigzag or a spiral), and the chat's button now waits until it lands.
