Oruinwan is live at oruinwan.poelys.com, and every app now connects to it out of the box.

- Continue with Google on Android phones and tablets, and in the Mac, Windows and Linux apps (Google opens in your browser and brings you back)
- The apps connect to the Oruinwan server straight away, with no address to type
- Light mode across the web, desktop and tablet apps, with the new glossy look
- A short tour for new accounts, with Next and Skip
- Threads open in split view next to your conversation; huddles and calls work end to end
- Admin dashboards never show anyone's messages: activity is counted from tasks, files and docs only
- Files with accents, emoji or other special characters in their names now upload
- Install on a Mac: open the DMG, drag Oruinwan to Applications, then the first time go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway
