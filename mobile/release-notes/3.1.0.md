Oruinwan 3.1 is a big refresh of the phone and tablet app, with matching improvements on the web. Phones install it as a full update once.

- **Chats:** one list of channels, DMs, groups and agents, newest first, with WhatsApp-style times. Pin up to 3 chats, archive, mute, mark as read or delete with a long press. Photos and files show as "📷 Photo" instead of "Encrypted message".
- **Search in place:** the search button grows across the header and shows results for the tab you're on (chats and people on Chats, tasks on Tasks, notes on Notes), then "See all results".
- **Conversations:** swipe a message right to reply, tap it for its thread, double tap to react. Tap a reaction to see who reacted, add several reactions, and jump to any date from the date chip. DMs show sent and read ticks (read receipts can be turned off in Privacy). Formatting shows as you type. Ask AI about a chat from the pill at the top.
- **Send later, drafts and sent:** press and hold Send to schedule a message. Find drafts, scheduled and sent messages in the side panel.
- **Huddles:** add people to a call that has already started. Swipe the call down to keep using the app. The "Meeting ended" banner closes by itself.
- **Navigation:** Back returns to where you came from. The AI button floats above the + button. Drag the line above the tab bar for more places, and choose which 5 places sit on the bar. Swipe in from the right edge (or tap your picture) for your profile, workspace, settings and help.
- **Home:** quick cards for unread, tasks, huddles, saved, drafts and scheduled messages, then the latest activity, your tasks, a Quick jump row you choose, and recent notes.
- **Activity calendar:** meetings, huddles, tasks due, reminders and scheduled messages on a calendar, on the phone and the web.
- **Tasks and notes:** the task view is a dropdown, and you can drag cards between board columns (with Undo). AI summaries and action items are written straight into the note. Empty huddle notes are no longer kept.
- **Links:** copy links to channels, notes and tasks. They open the app, or the website after sign-in. The website now says when a link is from a workspace you're not in.
- **Web:** a slimmer sidebar with a More menu, and Add people during calls. Channels are simply public or private.
