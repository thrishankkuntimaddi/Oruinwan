The first release published from GitHub. The app is the same as 1.0.1:

- In-app updates now work on Android 9 and 10 (and early Android 13 builds)
- A brief server outage no longer signs you out, and signing out fully ends your session
- Live updates reconnect on their own; Oruinwan AI renews an expired session
- New encryption keys are no longer readable by the server without your password
- "Clear this device" also removes cached encryption keys
- Phones with an outdated Android System WebView see how to update it instead of a blank screen
