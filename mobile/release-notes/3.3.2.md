Oruinwan 3.3.2 makes starting conversations work like Slack everywhere and tidies the phone app. It's a quick update on phones and the desktop app; no reinstall.

- **New message with a To: bar, everywhere:** Home's compose button and the Direct messages "+" open it too, and the phone app has it. Add one person and your chat with them opens; add more and your group with exactly those people opens, or you can start it (with a name if you like).
- **Message yourself:** pick yourself in the To: bar for notes, links and drafts only you can see.
- **Group "+":** next to a group's name, it opens the To: bar with the group's people, so you can add or remove someone and see that conversation instead.
- **Mention offers know your groups:** mentioning someone in a 1:1 chat offers to open your existing group with them instead of starting another.
- **Phone:** the "more places" arrow sits in the middle of the tab bar; the new message, task and note buttons are just icons; dates no longer pile up at the top of a chat; "Ask AI about this chat" stays a small button; the quick AI window keeps your conversation when you expand it and has a New chat button.
