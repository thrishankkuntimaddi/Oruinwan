Oruinwan 3.11.0 adds home-screen widgets on Android. It is a full download (the widgets are part of the app itself), offered by the in-app updater.

- **Chats widget:** your unread and latest conversations with message previews and unread counts. Tap one to open it.
- **Tasks widget:** your tasks that are overdue or due soon, with a + to add one.
- **Huddles widget:** who is in a live huddle right now, your next meeting, and a Start a huddle button.
- **Quick actions widget:** your pinned chats and the people you talk to most, plus Message, Task, Note and Huddle buttons.

Add them from your home screen: touch and hold an empty spot, then Widgets → Oruinwan. They are glass cards that follow the app's light or dark theme, and they update whenever you use the app. Signing out empties them.

Also in this release (from 3.10.x): smoother scrolling and screen changes on phones and tablets, and Settings in the same glass layout everywhere.
