Oruinwan 3.2.1 adds slash commands and smooth motion everywhere. It's a quick update on phones and the desktop app; no reinstall.

- **Slash commands:** type / in any message box (channels, DMs, groups and threads) for a list of commands. /task, /remind, /poll, /checklist, /huddle, /call, /ai, /summarize, /later, /invite, /leave, /mute, /status, /away, /back, /topic, /search and more, the same on the web and the phone. /help lists them all.
- **Times you can just type:** "in 2 hours", "5pm", "tomorrow 9am", "friday", "2026-11-02".
- **Remind others:** /remind @mani tomorrow 9am send the report reminds Mani, and everyone sees the line.
- **Your status:** /status 🌴 On holiday shows on your profile card.
- **Mentions:** mentioning someone in a DM opened from the Messages page now offers a group; in a group, mentioning someone outside it offers to add them.
- **Motion:** chats, panels, dialogs, new messages, tabs and the drawer animate softly and quickly. Turn on reduced motion in your system settings and none of it plays.
- **Send later** from the website, with /later.
