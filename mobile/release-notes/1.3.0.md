Create an account from your phone, and light or dark that follows your device.

- Phones: "Create an account" on the sign-in screen. Your server emails a link to confirm your address, then you sign in as usual
- Creating or joining a workspace from the phone comes in a later update
- Computers and tablets now follow your device's light or dark setting until you pick one, and signing in no longer switches it
- The sign-in screen on computers and tablets only shows Google, GitHub or LinkedIn when your server has them set up
- New passwords need 8 to 16 characters with an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol, on every app
- Some of this needs your team's server on the latest version
