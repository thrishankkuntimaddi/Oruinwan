Oruinwan 2.2 brings a real call screen everywhere and much calmer notifications. This is a full app update (the desktop and Android apps changed), so install it when asked.

- Calls: every huddle opens a full call screen with video tiles, controls and the meeting panel. Minimise it to a small floating call and keep using the app
- Desktop app: the call floats in its own small window on top of other apps, and screen sharing works
- Android: video calls, a full-screen call view with a floating bubble, flip camera and speaker toggle. Calls keep running in the background with Mute and Leave in the notification. The app explains before asking for the microphone or camera
- One call at a time: joining another call asks "Stay or switch?"
- Move a call between your devices: "In a call on MacBook · Move here" on your phone moves it with a short pause, keeping mute and camera
- Notifications: nothing for the chat you're reading, on any device. Messages from one person are grouped ("Ben · 5 new messages"). Reading a chat clears its notifications everywhere. Do not disturb now silences alerts
- Email: no more "new sign-in" email every time you sign back in on the same device, and you can turn it off. The digest is now Smart by default: a daily email only when something waited for you, otherwise a weekly roundup
- Limits: screen sharing from an Android phone isn't available yet (you can see others' screens); moving a call has a 1–2 second pause
