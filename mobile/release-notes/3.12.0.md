Oruinwan 3.12.0 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Two designs:** Liquid Glass stays the default. Settings → Appearance → Design now also offers **Unslop**: quiet surfaces, one bronze accent from the logo, nothing see-through. Your choice is saved on each device.
- **Background colour for Glass:** turn it off to put the glass on plain grey (light) or black (dark) instead of the soft colour field.
