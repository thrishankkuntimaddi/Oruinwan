Oruinwan 3.10.0 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Liquid Glass:** a new look on the phone, tablet, desktop app and website, inspired by Apple's Liquid Glass. A soft colour wallpaper sits behind clear glass panels; headers, the tab bar, menus and dialogs are frosted glass that content scrolls under. Buttons are rounded capsules, and the main button is white glass. Colour now only means something: red for unread and today, green for switches and success. Everything is where it was.
- **Tablet layout:** the list and the open item are two rounded glass panes, and the tab bar is a floating glass dock under both. More in the dock opens every other place (Files, Calendar, Settings…).
- **Light by default, as before.** Dark is the same glass, smoked. Pick it in Settings → Appearance.
