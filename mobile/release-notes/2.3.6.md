Oruinwan 2.3.6 makes sure everyone in a workspace can message everyone, on every device. This is a quick update: no reinstall.

- Members can message other members again, not only the owner and admins ("This conversation's encryption key is not ready yet" is fixed)
- Works the same whether you sign in with Google or with a password, on the web, desktop and phone apps
- Someone who hasn't signed in yet gets your messages as soon as they do
- The phone app picks up your current keys if you set up encryption again on another device
