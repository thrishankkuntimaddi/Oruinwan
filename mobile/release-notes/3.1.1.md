Oruinwan 3.1.1 adds pages to notes and whiteboards, and reminders for notes. It is a quick update on the phone and the desktop.

- **Note pages:** a note can have several pages. Tap a page under the title to open it, + to add one, or swipe sideways between them. Pages share the note's sharing.
- **Whiteboard pages:** a meeting's whiteboard can have several pages, each drawn on live by everyone in the meeting.
- **Remind me about a note:** pick a time in the note's menu to get a notification. Note reminders also show in Activity → Calendar.
- **Fix:** Delete note on the phone now deletes the note instead of archiving it.
