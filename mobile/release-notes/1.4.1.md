Oruinwan is coming to Google Play.

- The Mac, Windows and Linux apps open to the sign-in screen again instead of an empty window
- Android: a Google Play build that updates through Play (the GitHub download still updates itself)
- Invitation links point at oruinwan.poelys.com, so they can open in the Android app
- A public page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data: oruinwan.poelys.com/delete-account
- Install on a Mac: open the DMG, drag Oruinwan to Applications, then the first time go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway
