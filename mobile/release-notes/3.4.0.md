Oruinwan 3.4.0 fixes a long list of things on the phone and adds a few big ones. On Android this is a full app update (new native code for notifications and calls); the in-app updater installs it.

- **Messages in notifications:** Android shows the message itself, even when the app is closed. Messages stay end-to-end encrypted: the phone decrypts them, never the server. Home and Activity show the message text too, cut to one line.
- **Calls:** leave the app during a call and it shrinks into a floating picture-in-picture window (Android).
- **Chats:** select several chats to message, mark read, mute, archive or delete them at once; a pinned-messages bar; Unpin works; drafts are kept when you leave a chat; reacting no longer opens the thread; ticks line up; channel admins in channel details; agents answer inside threads.
- **Photos and videos:** swipe through all of a chat's media, with Share, Open / save and Show in chat; videos have thumbnails.
- **Calendar:** opens on the month; add personal reminders with +. Activity opens on activity, with a dot on the calendar when something is coming up.
- **Oruinwan AI:** the button has moods (happy, surprised, sad, sleepy, tongue out) and the quick AI window knows what's on screen ("Summarise this chat").
- **Workspace:** owner and admins in the drawer, Transfer ownership in Admin, starter templates and Save as a template, Members & roles, a fixed Permissions screen, and channel posting settings that show their choice.
- **Plans:** Free, Pro and Enterprise side by side, with support and Enterprise contact.
- **Integrations and agents:** connect apps on the phone, with real logos. Automations start from a template on the phone.
- **Files and notes:** star, archive, share and delete files; long-press or select notes; Ask the knowledge base.
- **Also:** Settings grouped clearly, windows always open on top, Terms and Privacy open the website, simpler sign-up text, and Use a different account goes straight to sign-in.
