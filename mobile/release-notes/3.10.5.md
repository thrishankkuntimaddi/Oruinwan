Oruinwan 3.10.5 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Smoother everywhere:** scrolling, opening screens and switching tabs on phones and tablets do less work per frame; screens hidden behind the one you are on stop drawing, and the glass bars use a cheaper effect.
- **Pages glide in:** on the web and the desktop app, moving between sections fades the new page in instead of swapping it abruptly; dialogs open more smoothly.
- **Settings on a phone browser** has the same floating glass header and tab bar as the app.
