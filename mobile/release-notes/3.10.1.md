Oruinwan 3.10.1 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Smoother:** the More drawer above the tab bar now slides in smoothly, and the profile panel, workspace menu and sheets open without stutter on phones.
- **Glass everywhere:** lists and cards on the phone sit on clear glass; every screen header, including conversations and threads, is the same floating glass bar.
- **Cleaner sign-in:** no header bar or AI button before you sign in, and no box inside the email field.
- **On the web:** pop-ups (Create channel, Schedule meeting, Broadcast, workspace settings, the AI panel's About and History) are solid frosted glass, so nothing behind them shows through. Switches are green, and the remaining square buttons and fields are rounded.
- **Fix:** "Open in the main view" on the AI split view opens the AI panel instead of the website.
