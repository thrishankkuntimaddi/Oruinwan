Oruinwan 3.8.4 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **The AI button where it belongs:** Home, Chats, Tasks, Notes, Files, Knowledge and Huddles keep the floating AI button, and a chat keeps its own. Other screens (threads, a task, a note, the roadmap, calendar, automations and more) show it in the header, and the floating button flies up into it and back down when you leave.
- **Tablets, portrait and landscape:** the list and the open item side by side, with every place in the bottom bar under both (no sidebar). The bar never covers the message box.
