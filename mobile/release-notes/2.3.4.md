Oruinwan 2.3.4 brings a new call layout that works for 3, 10 or 25 people, on the phone, tablet, desktop and web. It installs as a quick update.

- Grid view that fits everyone on screen: tiles resize to the space, and when there are more people than fit, swipe or use the arrows to see the next page. Nobody gets lost
- Speaker view: the person talking is shown large, everyone else in a strip
- Pin anyone: tap or press and hold a person (or a shared screen) to keep them large; Unpin to go back
- Screen shares take the main screen for everyone automatically
- 1:1 calls: the other person fills the screen and your camera sits in a small corner window you can drag
- Smoother big calls: only the videos you can see are played, while you still hear everyone
- Talking, muted and raised-hand indicators on every tile
