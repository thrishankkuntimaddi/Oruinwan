Oruinwan 2.3.5 gives the app a light icon that matches the new look. Because the icon is part of the installed app, this update is a full download, once.

- A light Oruinwan icon on your home screen, dock and taskbar
- Android 13+: turn on themed icons in your phone's settings and Oruinwan follows your colours
- iPhone and iPad (iOS 18+): the icon follows your home screen's light, dark or tinted look
- Desktop: while Oruinwan is open in dark mode, its dock / taskbar icon turns dark too
