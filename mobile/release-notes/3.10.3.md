Oruinwan 3.10.3 is a quick update: the in-app updater installs it without a full download.

- **Every pop-up is solid glass:** dialogs, menus, profile cards, the Ask AI panel, channel settings and dropdowns are clean light glass. Nothing behind them shows through, and the search bar no longer sits on top of dialogs.
- **Settings** has rounded buttons and fields and a clear top bar.
- **Rounded everywhere:** the last square buttons, fields and filters (task options, automation templates, docs search, blog filters, phone dropdowns) are rounded.
- **Nothing invisible on the phone:** progress bars, selection highlights and outlines that had faded into the glass are visible again.
- Focus rings are soft and rounded, and the Ask AI title no longer shows "##".
