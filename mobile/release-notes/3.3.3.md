Oruinwan 3.3.3 brings a real calendar, workspace pictures and a tidier way around your conversations. It's a quick update on phones and the desktop app; no reinstall.

- **Calendar:** day, week, month and schedule views, like Google Calendar. Meetings, huddles, tasks due, reminders and scheduled messages sit at their times on an hour grid with a line at "now". Filter by kind, and tap an empty time to schedule a meeting there.
- **Workspace picture:** owners and admins can give the workspace a picture, cropped to a square like a group icon. It shows in the workspace switcher and headers.
- **Profile photo on the phone:** change or remove your photo right in the app, cropped to a square.
- **Quick jump (phone Home):** scroll sideways through your recent chats and long-press to pin; pinned chats, groups and channels stay first.
- **Newest first:** channels, direct messages and groups are listed by latest activity everywhere, and move up as messages arrive.
- **Website and desktop:** click a chat's name for its details; Messages opens on New message; Tasks opens on All tasks; a phone-narrow browser window switches to the phone layout.
