Oruinwan 3.3.4 makes the phone app easier to find your way around. It's a quick update on phones and the desktop app; no reinstall.

- **Workspace drawer:** tap the workspace at the top left, or swipe in from the left edge, to open the workspace: its cover and picture, switch workspace, details, invite people, Calendar, Huddles, Files, Knowledge and Admin.
- **Cover and picture on the phone:** owners and admins add or change the workspace cover photo and picture right from the drawer, Workspace details or Admin → General. Pictures are cropped to a square.
- **Your side:** your profile picture at the top right opens your profile, account and settings.
- **Calendar:** opens on Schedule, with one simple toolbar: Today, back and forward, the view, and a filter for what to show. Calendar is also in More and in the Activity header.
- **Oruinwan AI button:** sits above the + button and does a small hop when you change pages.
- **Clearer buttons:** bolder + and New message buttons.
- **Privacy policy:** lists the service providers we use (hosting, push, email, AI, Google sign-in).
